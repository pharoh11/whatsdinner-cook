Weeknight Turkey Chili

Serves 6 · Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 lb ground turkey (93% lean)
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 (15 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • Shredded cheddar, sour cream and sliced green onions, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook, stirring often, until softened, 5 to 6 minutes. Stir in the garlic and cook 30 seconds more.
  2. Add the turkey and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook until no longer pink, about 6 minutes.
  3. Sprinkle in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano and salt. Stir for 1 minute so the spices toast in the fat.
  4. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and broth, then add both cans of beans and the brown sugar. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce the heat to low, partially cover, and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes so nothing sticks to the bottom.
  6. Taste and adjust the salt. If it’s too thick, splash in a little more broth.
  7. Ladle into bowls and top with cheddar, sour cream and green onions.
